### 03 / How the Work Begins

**The work starts before the first pixel.**

Before the layout, there is a question. Before the polish, there is a direction.

1. **Reception** — Gather the input, context and real problem.
2. **Distillation** — Analyze, define and remove the noise.
3. **Focus** — Clarify the message and align the goal.
4. **Path & Direction** — Plan the structure and choose the right system.
5. **Execution** — Build, refine and prepare the work to scale.

### 04 / The Standard

**Good-looking isn't the finish line.**

A strong project needs more than polish. It needs clarity, consistency, scalability and impact. These are the standards used to decide when the work is actually finished.
